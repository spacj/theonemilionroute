## How Can I Ensure My AI-Assisted Songs Qualify for Copyright Protection?

To secure copyright protection for AI-assisted music, creators must demonstrate substantial human authorship in their songs. This means going beyond entering prompts: arranging melody, writing lyrics, adding instrumental parts, or creatively editing AI outputs significantly strengthens the claim. Registration isn’t mandatory, but documenting your artistic choices and modifications helps prove your creative role if disputes arise.
